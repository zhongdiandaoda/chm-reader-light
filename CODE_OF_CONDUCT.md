# Code of Conduct

CHMReaderLight welcomes users and contributors who want local CHM reading on macOS to be dependable, private, and pleasant.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps the project:

- Be respectful of different experience levels, languages, and operating environments.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable, and focused on the app or documentation.
- Share reproduction details without exposing private document content, credentials, or personal paths.
- Assume maintainers and contributors are working in good faith.

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Dismissing reports because they involve unfamiliar CHM authoring tools, languages, or macOS versions.
- Repeated off-topic comments after maintainers ask to refocus the discussion.

## Reporting Problems

If a conduct issue happens in a public thread, flag it in the thread or open a minimal issue that links to the public discussion.

If a report includes private information or a security concern, follow [Security Policy](./SECURITY.md) instead of posting details publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or hide comments, close issues, block participants, or take other reasonable steps to keep project spaces useful and respectful.
